## Description

Every AI coding assistant that reads your logs, checks your commits, or talks to your CI/CD pipeline is (probably) doing it through MCP — the Model Context Protocol. Most explainers stop at "it's USB-C for AI." This one goes further: the protocol's biggest change didn't happen at its November 2024 launch — it happened in July 2026, when MCP went stateless and became something you can actually run behind a standard load balancer.

In this episode:
- What MCP is and the M×N integration problem it solves
- The host/client/server architecture, from a DevOps point of view
- Why the July 2026 stateless spec rewrite matters more than the original launch
- The real adoption timeline: Anthropic → OpenAI → Google DeepMind → Microsoft/GitHub → the Agentic AI Foundation

Part of a series on Cloud, DevOps, DevSecOps, Agentic AI, AI Agents, MCP, and AIOps — follow for the next one.
